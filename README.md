# DIMENSION · 维度

**The Infinite Architecture of Reality** — a civilization-scale, bilingual (EN / 中文) interactive cathedral of dimensions. From the dimensionless point (0D) to eleven-dimensional M-theory and beyond, DIMENSION explores spatial, temporal, quantum, mathematical, conscious and informational dimensions — and the possibility that reality is a projection from higher dimensions.

Built as an immersive single-page experience: a cinematic landing that opens from complete darkness, an interactive 0D→11D ascent, real-time tesseract and Calabi–Yau manifold visualizations, interactive equations, a cursor-warped consciousness lattice, a black-hole / wormhole spacetime fabric, speculative dimensional civilizations, an AI latent-space, a 7-axis comparative meta-model, a procedural cosmic soundscape, and a scroll-driven collapse into white light. Hidden portal included (↑↑↓↓←→←→ b a).

## Sections

1. **The Evolution of Dimensions** — interactive 0D→11D journey (point → line → plane → solid → tesseract → branching worlds → … → superstrings → M-theory)
2. **Dimensional Mathematics** — rotatable 4D tesseract, animated Calabi–Yau cross-section, interactive equations (quartic, hypersphere, Einstein field equations, fractal dimension, quantum state)
3. **Dimensions & Consciousness** — a lattice that bends toward your cursor, with emerging quotes
4. **Dimensions & Physics** — spacetime fabric with black hole, wormhole pair and a cursor-tracking gravity well
5. **Dimensional Civilizations** — speculative societies native to 2D, 4D, 7D and ∞D
6. **The Dimension of AI** — intelligence as dimensional compression, a rotating latent space
7. **The Edge of Dimensions** — a scroll-driven fracture, white-out and final line
